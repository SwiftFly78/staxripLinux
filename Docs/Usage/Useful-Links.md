## StaxRipLinux

- [Releases](https://github.com/SwiftFly78/staxripLinux/releases): downloads and the changes of every version
- [Issue tracker](https://github.com/SwiftFly78/staxripLinux/issues): bug reports and feature requests
- [StaxRip](https://github.com/staxrip/staxrip), which StaxRipLinux is based on, and its
  [community on Discord](https://discord.gg/uz8pVR79Bd)

## Encoding

- [Doom9 forum](https://forum.doom9.org): the forum about video encoding, with threads about every encoder and filter
- [VideoHelp forum](https://forum.videohelp.com) and its [software](https://www.videohelp.com/software) pages
- [Encoding guide by Silentaperture](https://silentaperture.gitlab.io/mdbook-guide/) about encoding with VapourSynth

## Encoders

- x264: [repository](https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264), `x264 --fullhelp` lists all options
- x265: [documentation](https://x265.readthedocs.io/en/master/cli.html)
- SVT-AV1: [parameters](https://gitlab.com/AOMediaCodec/SVT-AV1/-/blob/master/Docs/Parameters.md)
- aomenc: [AV1 documentation](https://aomedia.googlesource.com/aom/)
- rav1e: [repository](https://github.com/xiph/rav1e)
- vvenc: [usage](https://github.com/fraunhoferhhi/vvenc/wiki/Usage)
- NVEncC: [options](https://github.com/rigaya/NVEnc/blob/master/NVEncC_Options.en.md)
- QSVEncC: [options](https://github.com/rigaya/QSVEnc/blob/master/QSVEncC_Options.en.md)
- VCEEncC: [options](https://github.com/rigaya/VCEEnc/blob/master/VCEEncC_Options.en.md)
- FFmpeg: [documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/documentation.html), the
  [H.264](https://trac.ffmpeg.org/wiki/Encode/H.264) and [H.265](https://trac.ffmpeg.org/wiki/Encode/H.265) guides and
  [hardware acceleration with VA-API](https://trac.ffmpeg.org/wiki/Hardware/VAAPI)
